Outlook on the web or new Outlook (Mac or Windows)
  1. Open Outlook on the web (outlook.office.com) and click the gear icon → Calendar → Shared calendars.
  2. Under Publish a calendar, choose Calendar and set the permission to Can view all details, so titles and locations are included. Then click Publish.
  3. Two links appear. Copy the ICS link and paste it into your browser. A .ics file downloads.
  4. In Calendar Planning, click Load .ics and pick that file.

Year template

28 Sep 2026 to 31 Dec 2027, one column per day. Exported as YEAR_TEMPLATE_AGENDA.xlsx with the same layout.

How events are placed

  1. Row from the Outlook category. An event goes to the row named like one of its categories: J&L OOO, SS PRECO, SS PRECO 2, FW PRECO, SS SHOW, FW SHOW, PAULA.
  2. Otherwise, from the subject. "SS PRECO: Design review" goes to SS PRECO and shows "Design review". "SS PRECO | text" works too.
  3. Days covered. Multi-day and all-day events fill every day they cover. Timed events go on their start day. Recurring series are expanded.
  4. Same day, same row. Texts are stacked on separate lines in one cell.
  5. Skipped. Cancelled events and anything outside 28 Sep 2026 to 31 Dec 2027.

Not placed

Events with no matching category or subject prefix.

In the real version, the planner would read the Outlook calendar and add the new rows to the tracker on SharePoint directly, with no copy and paste. That needs IT to approve access to Microsoft 365 (Graph API or Power Automate).
